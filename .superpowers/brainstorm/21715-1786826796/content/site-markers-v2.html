How should flying sites look on the planning map?

This is the Fly tab — before you start a flight. Weather-on-tap comes later; this is just how spots appear.

A · Quiet dots

Small brand marks. Terrain stays readable. Names wait for a later tap or closer zoom.

B · Named pins

Name + takeoff altitude on the map. Easier to plan at a glance; busier when many sites overlap.

C · Takeoff vs landing

Triangle for takeoff, disc for landing. Type is obvious; names still wait for tap.
